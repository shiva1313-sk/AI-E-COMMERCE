# Delivery and Shipping Information

## 1. Shipping Options and Timelines
We deliver to over 19,000 postal pincodes across India through premier courier partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and India Post).

- **Standard Delivery**: 
  - Estimated delivery within **3 to 5 business days** for metro cities and tier-1 locations.
  - **4 to 7 business days** for tier-2, tier-3, and remote locations.
  - Delivery is **FREE** on all orders above ₹999. A flat nominal shipping fee of ₹49 applies to orders under ₹999.
- **Express / Next-Day Delivery**:
  - Available in select metropolitan cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune).
  - Orders placed before 1:00 PM are dispatched on the same day and delivered within **24 to 48 hours**.
  - Flat fee of ₹99 applies regardless of cart value.

## 2. Order Tracking
- Once your order is dispatched from our warehouse, you will receive an automated email and SMS notification containing the Courier Partner Name and AWB Tracking Number.
- You can track your package in real-time by visiting **My Orders > Track Shipment** or directly on the courier partner's tracking portal.

## 3. Delivery Attempts and Verification
- Our delivery executives will make up to **3 delivery attempts** before returning the package to our warehouse.
- For high-value electronics and smartphones (orders exceeding ₹10,000), a secure **OTP (One-Time Password)** will be sent to your registered mobile number for verification at the time of delivery.
- Please do not share the OTP until you have physically verified the intact condition of the outer shipping box. If the outer package appears tampered or open, refuse delivery immediately.
