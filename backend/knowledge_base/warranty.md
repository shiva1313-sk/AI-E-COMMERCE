# Product Warranty Policy

## 1. Standard Brand Warranty
- All electronic products (Smartphones, Laptops, Keyboards, Audio Devices, Smart Watches, PC Peripherals) sold on our platform carry a minimum **1-Year Manufacturer Brand Warranty** from the date of invoice.
- Selected premium components and backpacks carry extended manufacturer warranties:
  - Backpacks and Travel Gear: **2-Year Limited Stitch & Zipper Warranty**.
  - Laptop mechanical switches / keyboard switches: **1-Year Limited Replacement Guarantee**.

## 2. What Is Covered Under Warranty?
- Manufacturing defects in materials and craftsmanship.
- Hardware failure under normal recommended operating conditions.
- Sensor, battery, or motherboard malfunctions not caused by external trauma or abuse.
- Dead-on-arrival (DOA) units reported within 48 hours of initial delivery.

## 3. What Is Excluded From Warranty?
- Physical damage, drops, cracks, dents, and accidental liquid/water immersion (unless certified IP rating explicitly covers it).
- Wear and tear resulting from routine daily usage (scratches on display, fabric fraying, normal cosmetic wear).
- Unauthorized disassembly, modifications, unofficial firmware flashing, or repairs conducted by third-party technicians.
- Damage caused by electrical surges, faulty wall sockets, or non-certified third-party charging cables.

## 4. How to Claim Warranty
1. Locate your tax invoice in your account under **My Orders > Download Invoice**.
2. Visit any authorized manufacturer brand service center across India with the original invoice and product serial number.
3. Alternatively, contact our dedicated warranty desk at `warranty@shopease.com` or call `1800-123-4567` for doorstep warranty pickup in select metro cities.
