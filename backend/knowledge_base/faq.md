# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## 1. General Ordering & Account Questions

### How do I check my order status?
You can check your order status anytime by logging into your account and clicking on **My Orders**. Click on the specific order to view current progress (Processing, Packed, Dispatched, Out for Delivery, Delivered) and live courier tracking.

### Can I modify my shipping address after placing an order?
You can edit your delivery address directly from the **My Orders** screen as long as the order has not been dispatched. Once dispatched, addresses cannot be altered in transit due to courier security constraints.

### How do I get an official GST tax invoice?
Every shipped order automatically generates a GST-compliant tax invoice. Go to **My Orders > Order Details > Download Invoice (PDF)**.
