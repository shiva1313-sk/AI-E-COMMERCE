# Order Cancellation Policy

## 1. Cancellation Before Dispatch
- Customers can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it has been dispatched from our fulfillment warehouse.
- To cancel an order before dispatch:
  1. Go to **My Orders**.
  2. Select the relevant order.
  3. Click **Cancel Order** and select a cancellation reason.
- For prepaid orders cancelled prior to dispatch, the full refund is initiated automatically within **2 hours**, and funds are credited back to the original payment source within **24 to 48 hours** for UPI/Wallets or **3 to 5 business days** for credit/debit cards.

## 2. Cancellation After Dispatch
- If the order has already been shipped or is in transit, it cannot be cancelled directly through the website or mobile app.
- In this case, customers have two options:
  - **Refuse delivery** when the courier delivery agent attempts doorstep delivery.
  - **Accept delivery** and initiate a 7-day return through the standard return portal.
- Once the package is marked as returned to sender (RTO), a full refund will be processed minus any expedited shipping charges.

## 3. Merchant / System Cancellations
We reserve the right to cancel an order under the following exceptional circumstances:
- The ordered product is unexpectedly out of stock or fails final pre-dispatch quality inspection.
- The delivery address is incomplete, unserviceable, or falls outside our courier coverage zones.
- Flagged fraudulent transactions or pricing errors due to system glitches.
- In such cases, customers will be notified immediately via email and SMS, and a 100% full refund will be issued immediately.
