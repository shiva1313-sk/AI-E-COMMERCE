# Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Window and Eligibility
- Customers can return products within **7 days** of delivery for eligible items.
- To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original, unused condition, with all original tags, user manuals, warranty cards, and intact packaging.
- Electronics (Smartphones, Laptops, Keyboards, Audio Devices) must be factory reset with all personal accounts removed.
- Defective, damaged, or incorrect items received must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery with unboxing photos or video evidence.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned unless received in a damaged or defective condition:
- Opened software, digital codes, or downloadable products.
- Consumable items where the hygienic seal has been broken (e.g., in-ear earbuds ear-tips, cleaning cloths).
- Products marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance".
- Items returned without serial numbers, original invoice, or with damaged manufacturer barcodes.

## 3. Return Process
1. Navigate to **My Orders** in your account dashboard and click **Request Return**.
2. Select the reason for return and upload supporting photographs if the product is damaged or defective.
3. Our courier partner will schedule a doorstep quality check and pickup within **2 to 3 business days**.
4. Once the returned item reaches our fulfillment center and passes inspection (1-2 days), the refund will be triggered immediately.

## 4. Refund Methods and Timelines
Refunds will be processed back to the original payment method according to the following schedule:
- **UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)**: 24 to 48 hours after return verification.
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: 3 to 7 business days depending on the issuing bank.
- **Net Banking**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Refund will be credited via NEFT / IMPS bank transfer or instant Store Wallet Credit within 2 business days after the customer provides their bank details.
- **Shipping Fees**: Standard shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a merchant error (defective or wrong item delivered).
