# Payment Information and Methods

## 1. Supported Payment Options
We provide secure, encrypted 256-bit SSL checkout supporting the following payment methods:
- **UPI**: Instant payment via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and all bank UPI apps with zero transaction surcharge.
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Maestro, and American Express cards issued by all major domestic and international banks.
- **Net Banking**: Direct bank transfer supported for 50+ banks across India (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, etc.).
- **EMI & Pay Later**:
  - No-Cost EMI and Standard Credit Card EMI available on orders above ₹3,000 for tenures of 3, 6, 9, and 12 months.
  - Debit Card EMI and Cardless EMI available through partner providers.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**:
  - COD is available on orders between ₹299 and ₹20,000 for serviceable pincodes.
  - A nominal ₹30 handling charge is applicable for COD orders. Exact cash or QR code scan-on-delivery is accepted.

## 2. Payment Security and Encryption
- All transactions are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.
- We never store customer CVVs, credit/debit card numbers, or banking PINs on our servers.
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA / OTP) is mandatory for all card transactions.

## 3. Failed or Pending Payments
- In the event of a transaction failure where money was debited from your bank account but no order confirmation was generated:
  - The payment gateway will automatically initiate a refund within **24 hours**.
  - Banks typically take **2 to 5 business days** to credit the amount back to your account.
  - If the amount is not reversed after 5 business days, please share the transaction reference ID (UTR number) with our customer support team for manual reconciliation.
